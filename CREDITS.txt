森羅物語：漁歌 / Kaleidoscope Cookery: Fishing Voyage
An unofficial add-on for the Kaleidoscope Cookery Bedrock Edition port.
Author: Garliccute

Requires
- Kaleidoscope Cookery (Bedrock port by Loyallay), version 1.6.1 or newer.
  Original Java project: Kaleidoscope Official Production Team
  https://github.com/KaleidoscopeMods/KaleidoscopeCookery

This add-on does not modify any Kaleidoscope Cookery files. It registers its recipes
and guidebook pages through Cookery's public Script Event API, and its Kombu Tea cups
reuse Cookery's teacup geometry by identifier and its cup_stack component, as the API
allows for add-on teas.

Art
The pack icon, the banner and the guidebook star icon are by the author, Garliccute.
All other textures and models in this pack are original pixel art drawn for this add-on.
They follow Kaleidoscope Cookery's visual style (16x16 item icons, hue-shifted
outlines, the same wooden-bowl and porcelain-plate colour ramps). Because the art is
deliberately styled after Cookery, whose original resources are licensed
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, this add-on's assets are released under the same licence.

Derived art: the Kombu Tea cup textures (textures/blocks/kfv_kombu_tea*.png and
textures/items/kfv_kombu_tea.png) are re-coloured copies of Kaleidoscope Cookery's
Barley Tea teacup textures (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, Kaleidoscope Official Production Team /
Loyallay's Bedrock port). Only the colours were changed.

Sound effects are original, synthesised for this add-on (tools/sounds.py).
Vibrant Visuals / RTX texture sets (MER and height maps) are generated from the
pack's own materials (tools/pbr.py).
